🌿 Welcome to Digital Detox Companion!

Your friendly guide for reducing screen time, rekindling real-world life, and making digital wellbeing feel refreshingly simple. This app is about mindful tech use and helping you build healthier online habits—without guilt or overwhelm.

Why was this app created?
Our world is full of digital distractions! Digital Detox Companion was made to support anyone seeking more balance: to boost presence, forge genuine connections, and rediscover joy away from endless scrolling. It’s designed for you to spend less time in the app and more time offline, in daily life.

  • Personalized Detox Plans: Set flexible time limits and offline goals that fit your lifestyle.
  • Accountability Buddy Pairing: Connect (anonymously) with a like-minded buddy for gentle support and mutual encouragement.
  • Real-World Rewards: Earn milestone rewards and real activity suggestions to replace screen time.
  • Off-Grid Check-Ins: Send friendly “I’m thinking of you” messages that only deliver when your buddy returns online.
  • AI Reflection Prompts: Get occasional, uplifting encouragement to reflect and track your progress—without being glued to your phone.
